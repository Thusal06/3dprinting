Printing under vacuum

Printing carbon-fibre composites under vacuum conditions reduced porosity from 13.9% to 4.1% while increasing flexural strength by almost 25%: process refinement, not a new material, narrowing the performance gap with conventional production.

A thousand parts become one

Rocket engine injectors conventionally required more than a thousand separate components and nine months to assemble. Printed, the same injector is a single piece produced in under two weeks at one tenth of the cost: fewer parts, less complexity, a shorter manufacturing chain.
